# spa-rv1909.json

- Text: Santa Biblia — Reina Valera 1909 (`SpaRV`)
- License of the text: public domain (1909 translation)
- Source: https://github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases (`formats/json/SpaRV.json`)
- Source repository license: MIT
- Converted to a single minified JSON file (whitespace removed, content unchanged)
- Edited in this project: 14 printer colophons ("Fué escrita de Corinto…", "Escrita de Roma…") removed from the last verse of the Pauline epistles. Orthography normalised to modern Spanish (preposition and conjunction accents, pre-1959 monosyllable accents, `Core`→`Coré`, `Johanan`→`Johanán`, `JESUS`/`JUDIOS` accented). The wording, punctuation and verse numbers are unchanged, and the verse numbers follow the 1909 division, which differs from the modern one where a chapter ends earlier (those slots are empty in the source and are skipped by `getVerses`).
